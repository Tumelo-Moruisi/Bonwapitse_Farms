About Our Farm

Bonwapitse Farm is located on tribal land in the Kgalagadi District, Botswana. Established in 2002, our farm spans vast open land where cattle roam freely without fences — truly free range, the way nature intended.

Our farm supplies quality beef to the Government of Botswana and offers a wide range of livestock including breeding bulls, horses, goats and sheep.
